Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice describes the current website operated by Vladimir Arjoca. Updated 25 September 2026. For privacy questions, email vladimir.arjoca@outlook.com.

Contact requests

The contact form opens your email application with the details you enter. It does not send a message automatically or store form submissions in a website database. If you send the email, your name, email, message and any optional company or phone details are used to respond to your request. Your email provider and the recipient’s email provider handle that message.

Language preference

Your selected language is saved in your browser under siteLanguage and included in page links. This preference contains no contact details. You can clear it using your browser’s site-data settings. If browser storage is unavailable, the language in the URL still works.

Hosting and technical data

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub’s privacy statement explains its handling of technical information.

Consultation bookings

The Schedule page embeds Cal.com. Loading the calendar connects your browser to Cal.com. If you make a booking, Cal.com processes the booking details you provide and makes the relevant details available to the organiser. Review Cal.com’s privacy policy before submitting a booking.

Assistant demo

The assistant currently provides local, predefined responses. Messages remain in memory in the current page and are not sent to an AI service by this demo. Reloading or leaving the page clears the conversation. Do not enter passwords, confidential configurations or incident evidence.

Store and payments

Products are currently marked Coming soon and purchases are disabled. The Store does not collect payment details. Lemon Squeezy is the planned checkout and delivery provider; the notice and applicable purchase terms must be updated when sales are enabled. External links follow the destination provider’s policies.

Privacy requests

For questions about a message or booking you have shared, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact vladimir.arjoca@outlook.com. Data held by external providers is also subject to their own privacy policies and request procedures.
