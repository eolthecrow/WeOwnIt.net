Network security assessment

Walk through the network from the outside in, then turn observations into a prioritized remediation list.

How these resources are built

Useful under pressure.

Each resource aims to make the next step clear for the operator, the reviewer, and the person accountable for the result.

01 / OBSERVE

Start with evidence

Record the scope, symptoms, timestamps, and known good state before drawing conclusions.

02 / DECIDE

Make ownership clear

Define who approves changes, who communicates, and when to escalate.

03 / VERIFY

Close the loop

Test the outcome, keep a record, and turn the lesson into a repeatable control.

What should a good checklist produce?

A named owner, a recorded finding, evidence, a priority, and a next action. A tick alone does not explain whether the risk was reduced.

How should I use the NIS2 checklist?

Use it to organize an internal gap review. Applicability and reporting obligations depend on your sector, size, and national implementation; confirm them with qualified legal or compliance advice.
